1.0.0 26 November 2026

The first version of PolyFox Portuguese: the Portuguese of Portugal, as a course of its own, made to the same standard as PolyFox French.

  • The whole course, A1 to C2: 180 units, 2,160 words and about 2,870 sentences, every one spoken by a Portugal voice.
  • Portugal, not Brazil. Brazilian words and forms, such as ônibus, estou fazendo or me chamo, are marked wrong with a note giving what is said in Portugal.
  • Spelling from before the 1990 agreement, such as acção or óptimo, is accepted, with today's spelling shown.
  • Tu with friends from the first unit, o senhor and a senhora from the second, and a tip on when você is all right.
  • Over 700 other correct wordings accepted. A wrong answer says why: o or a, um or uma, an ending, a Brazilian word, estar a, where the pronoun goes, an accent or the spelling.
  • Speaking is checked on the phone by a speech model inside the app, with a fairer pass mark for Portuguese. A different number or a missing não does not pass. Nothing is sent anywhere, and you can still mark yourself.
  • Twelve conversations set in Portugal, from a bica in a Lisbon café to the CP ticket office and a job interview, and 42 stories read aloud, six of them for beginners.
  • Verb tables for 385 verbs in every tense, with the personal infinitive, and a grammar book of all 362 rules.
  • Reviews timed by FSRS, the method behind Anki. Dictation, level exams, up to five people and a backup you keep.

PolyFoxPortuguese.apk is about 144 MB. Android 8 or later. SHA-256: 93e332d3099de673411971d373f3e48f7df2e46620c44bfd3601dcc9d625900b
