The first version of PolyFox Portuguese: the Portuguese of Portugal, as a course of its own.

- The whole course, A1 to C2: 180 units, 2,160 words and about 2,870 sentences, every one spoken by a Portugal voice.
- Portugal, not Brazil: Brazilian words and forms (ônibus, estou fazendo, me chamo) are marked wrong with a note giving what is said in Portugal.
- Spelling from before the 1990 agreement (acção, óptimo) is accepted, with today's spelling shown.
- Tu with friends from the first unit, o senhor and a senhora from the second, and a tip on when você is all right.
- Over 700 other correct wordings accepted; wrong answers explained (o or a, um or uma, endings, Brazilian words, estar a, pronoun position, accents).
- Speaking checked on the phone by a speech model inside the app, with a fairer pass mark for Portuguese. A different number or a missing não does not pass.
- Twelve conversations set in Portugal and 42 stories read aloud, six of them for beginners.
- Verb tables for 385 verbs with the personal infinitive, and a grammar book of all 362 rules.
- FSRS review timing, dictation, level exams, up to five people and a backup you keep. No internet permission.

PolyFoxPortuguese.apk, about 144 MB. Android 8 or later.
